AXISYMMETRIC SCALAR RESIDUAL REPAIR
14 September 2026

Issue
-----

The extra_fine_half run stopped when the relative scalar residual check divided a
subnormal absolute residual by a subnormal row scale. The scalar candidate was
otherwise valid. The failed snapshot is not a restart state; use the bundled
accepted checkpoint.

Correction
----------

The matrix, right-hand side, LU solve and three refinement steps are unchanged.
For a row with n stored matrix terms, the residual check permits an absolute
roundoff allowance of (2*n + 1) times the smallest float64 subnormal. This follows
from the row operations and is not a concentration cutoff. The relative tolerance,
budget checks and bounds checks are unchanged. Raw and adjusted residuals are both
recorded.

Restart compatibility
---------------------

The bundled checkpoint, budget CSV and initial field are unchanged copies of the
accepted files. Compatibility with the repaired source is limited to the reviewed
parent source; mesh, DOF, physical, timing and environment metadata must still
match. Historical source hashes remain in the restart record, and new checkpoints
record the repaired source.

Validation
----------

Local replay triggers the old failure and accepts the unchanged scalar candidate
with the corrected check. Injected errors in a populated cell and in the
small-concentration tail remain rejected. Before resuming on Blue Pebble, the
launcher repeats the scalar replay, coupled restart check and mesh/DOF identity
check.
